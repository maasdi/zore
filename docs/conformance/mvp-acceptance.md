# MVP acceptance audit

This is the issue #211 acceptance ledger against baseline `edcf5847b259f28ef0bbd1409a8c6751b39725cb`, with the bounded additions identified below. It is a source audit, not a declaration of complete MVP conformance or soundness. [The specification](../../spec/language-spec.md) remains authoritative. [mvp.md](mvp.md) preserves the historical inventory and dated corrections; [standard-library-evidence.md](standard-library-evidence.md) separately maps every §37 family, including external crypto/TLS adapters and host limitations.

Evidence means an executable assertion with a registered route. `autotests = false` in [compiler/Cargo.toml](../../compiler/Cargo.toml): the target must be explicitly listed. Below, `L`, `P`, `T`, `O`, `K`, `D`, `A`, and `N` mean respectively registered targets/files [lexer](../../tests/lexer/lexer.rs), [parser](../../tests/parser/parser.rs), [check](../../tests/typecheck/check.rs), [ownership](../../tests/ownership/ownership.rs), [packages](../../tests/packages/packages.rs), [source_diagnostics](../../tests/diagnostics/source_diagnostics.rs), [async_lowering](../../tests/async/lowering.rs), and [native](../../tests/codegen/native.rs). `N::module::test` is reached through `mod module;` in the native target; it is not a separate Cargo target. Names identify source assertions, not a claim that this audit ran them.

## Compact evidence index

Rows E01–E24 are shared by the feature, test-layer and invariant mappings. Acceptance helpers in T/O require both empty diagnostics and produced HIR; rejection helpers require no HIR and the named diagnostic substring. Native `prints` requires successful execution and exact stdout; panic helpers additionally check failure status/message. Consequently a name here must be read together with its stated assertion and boundary.

Conservative exclusions remain intentional: generic interfaces/named generic types, source lifetimes, raw pointers, unsafe, unrestricted FFI, cross-compilation and the other §40 forms are outside this acceptance scope. §41 TBD syntax does not become accepted language through an illustrative example.
